## Underkeep Executioner lock

- Riveted cylindrical execution mask with a narrow cyan slit; wet oxblood apron and asymmetric plate distinguish materials and rank.
- The prison-door guillotine hook-axe owns a broad real blade, rear hook, reinforced socket, wrapped haft and iron counterweight.
- Key silhouettes are vertical guard, advancing drag, overhead cleave, hooked pull, guillotine slam and kneeling collapse.
- Runtime art must preserve the complete axe in every frame and remain visibly larger than the seven normal enemies.

## Soul Gaoler Ormund lock

### Phase 1

- Disciplined 1.95× Player prison authority: closed barred prison crown, huge asymmetric armor, front/back soul cage, giant key ring, chains and complete Soul-Prison Key Halberd.
- Cage is physical machinery with doors, hinges, bars and contained souls, not an aura or flat chest emblem.

### Phase 2

- Cage doors and spine frame burst outward; crown splits around a drowned hollow face; torso grows and leans forward; chain roots and fused left arm/weapon change the silhouette.
- Phase 2 changes face, posture, cage volume, arm structure, weapon grip and locomotion. Cyan light supports the structure but does not substitute for it.

### Weapon and action continuity

- Key-halberd always retains its single-edged key blade, cage socket, wrapped haft and toothed counterweight.
- P1 actions read as disciplined heavy custody. P2 actions read as cage/chain/body fusion, while remaining physical and avoiding priest spellcasting or bullet spam.
- Transformation and death have authored multi-step structural poses. Runtime cannot swap textures after a flash or directly hide the Boss.
