# Edran Phase 1 concept provenance

The three source boards in this directory were generated specifically for *Veil of Ravenmourn* on 2026-07-29 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool from the approved original Edran design brief. They are project-owned production references and do not reproduce a licensed third-party character.

- `edran_phase_01_model_sheet.png`: front/side/back/silhouette model board.
- `edran_phase_01_equipment_board.png`: crown, vestment, crozier and censer construction board.
- `edran_phase_01_attack_pose_sheet.png`: Phase 1 pose and weapon-continuity board.

The smaller concept files are deterministic crops of those boards for focused review. Runtime pixel art is generated independently at 96×96 by `generate_edran_phase_01_art.gd`; the high-resolution boards are never downscaled and shipped as gameplay sprites.
