# Edran Boss Summon Assets

The two large concept boards are original project-specific generated references. They define silhouette, materials and animation intent only. Runtime PNGs are separately authored by the deterministic Godot `Image` generator at `scripts/tools/generate_edran_summon_art.gd` and are not resized concept images.

- `ossuary_penitent`: broad melee corpse; chained ossuary plates, unequal grave claws and penitential cloth.
- `choir_husk`: narrow ranged corpse; cracked funerary mask, throat bell, stitched seals and slow straight resonance projectile.

Neither summon contains loot data, encounter registration or persistence ownership.
