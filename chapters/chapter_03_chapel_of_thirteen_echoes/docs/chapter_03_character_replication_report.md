# Chapter III Character Replication Report

## Scope

The formal Chapter III roster was regenerated from the approved Chapel of Thirteen Echoes concepts: six standard enemies, Edran's two summons, and the Thirteenth Pontiff across both phases. Gameplay scripts, timings, collisions, room encounters, summon rules, and Boss balance remain unchanged.

## Formal art contract

- Standard enemies and summons: 128x128 transparent production canvases.
- The Thirteenth Pontiff: 192x192 transparent Boss canvases for pontifical Phase I, structural transformation, and black-bell Phase II.
- Critical identities: chain-and-bell penitence; execution censer; sewn-mouth chorister; leaded stained-glass wings; confessional lattice; scripture seal; ossuary burden; choir cage; Edran's thirteen-point crown, pontifical mask, black-bell chest reliquary, external ribs, crozier, and bound censer.
- Superseded frames are isolated under `archive_legacy/character_replication_pre95_v2/`; no formal scene references the archive.
