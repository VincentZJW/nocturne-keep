# Thirteenfold Absolution W4 — reward formation and reliquary acquisition

Status: implemented; automated and MainBootstrap QA passed

## Runtime flow

```text
Edran defeated (one-shot signal)
├── existing sanctum death environment
└── Chapter03RewardSequenceController (4.20 s)
    ├── fragments_converge
    ├── thirteen_seals_extinguish
    ├── blades_reforged
    └── weapon_ready
        ↓ both branches complete
PostBossExit opens
        ↓
Last Confession Reliquary
        ↓ E interaction
WeaponPickup → unique Inventory → auto-equip EquipmentManager
        ↓
acquisition panel + empty reliquary + Underkeep descent opens
```

The Boss remains the sole authority for AI shutdown, Hitbox cleanup and death. The reward controller owns only the post-death presentation and its temporary Player input/invulnerability lock. The post-Boss room owns room presentation; Inventory and Equipment remain the ownership and damage authorities.

## Formation contract

The approved 4.20-second sequence uses six restrained crozier/censer fragments, thirteen extinguishing seal nodes, the existing hollow-bell resonance and the formal W2 world weapon. It emits typed stage events for deterministic QA. A second `play_sequence()` call is rejected, so Edran cannot create multiple rewards.

Player input is locked and the Hurtbox is protected while the formation is active. Both values are restored to their prior state after completion. The post-Boss exit remains disabled until both the existing death environment and the reward formation have completed.

## Reliquary and transaction

The previous B6 story-token placeholder is replaced by a composed `WeaponPickup` using weapon ID `thirteenfold_absolution_blades`. The formal low reliquary uses chapter-local pixel assets, two candles, a visible pair of blades, a short bilingual prompt and a physical descent blocker.

Successful collection performs one transaction:

- adds exactly one unique owned weapon ID;
- auto-equips the W3 WeaponData;
- resolves normal/Dash damage to `14 / 28`;
- switches the Player's complete W2 visual to `thirteenfold_absolution`;
- sets `chapter_03_boss_reward_collected` and `chapter_03_underkeep_descent_unlocked`;
- marks Chapter III complete;
- replaces the display with the empty reliquary and opens the descent.

Reloading the room after collection keeps the reliquary empty and the descent open. Duplicate interaction is rejected by both the pickup state and the unique inventory ledger. The narrow W3 save service persists the owned/equipped weapon and story flags in formal play; Debug runs remain non-persistent.
