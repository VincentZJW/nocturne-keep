# Cursed Shield Guard Art Manifest

- Concept: broad corroded plate, raven heater shield, short sword, curse fissures.
- Formal animations: shielded and unshielded idle/walk/attack/hurt/death, plus `block` and `guard_break` (55 frames).
- Guard-break art removes the shield silhouette and uses dedicated metal-fracture effects.
- Distinction: widest ordinary silhouette; defensive state remains readable without relying on UI.
