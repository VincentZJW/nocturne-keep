# Fallen Crossbowman Art Manifest

- Concept: leather brigandine, partial helm/hood, stocky crossbow and visible quiver.
- Formal animations: `idle` 4, `walk` 6, `aim` 4, `shoot` 3, `reload` 4, `hurt` 3, `death` 6 (30 frames).
- Attack phases are explicit families: aim telegraph → bolt release → long mechanical reload.
- Distinction: lightest human enemy and only horizontal ranged weapon silhouette.
