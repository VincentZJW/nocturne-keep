# Gargoyle Sentinel Art Manifest

- Concept: carved raven-gargoyle, horn crown, folded stone wings, hooked claws and moss seams.
- Formal animations: dormant/wake/hover/dive windup/dive/ground stun/return/hurt/two-stage death (41 frames).
- Dive phases are separated into `dive_windup` and `dive`; death distinguishes fall from stone shatter.
- Distinction: non-human aerial diamond silhouette; never reads as an armored soldier.
