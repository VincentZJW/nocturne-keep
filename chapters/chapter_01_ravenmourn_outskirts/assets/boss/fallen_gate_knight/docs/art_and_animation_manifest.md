# Fallen Gate Knight Art Manifest

- Canvas: 96×96; formal animation total: 96 frames across 20 families.
- Phase 1: raven heater shield, sealed helm and disciplined stance.
- Phase 2: shield removed, open sword stance, exposed curse fissures and torn mantle.
- Dedicated turn, block, break, phase transition, charge, combo, overhead, jump-smash, shockwave, hurt and death art preserves all existing Boss contracts.
- See `chapter_01_boss_art_bible.md` for phase and silhouette rules.
