# Fallen Gate Knight Art Manifest

- Canvas: 128×96; the extra horizontal room belongs only to the Gatewarden Greatsword and preserves the previous body/feet world anchor. Formal animation total: 165 frames across 41 families.
- Phase 1: raven tower shield, sealed helm, disciplined stance and the 84–88%-of-body-height `Gatewarden Greatsword / 守门誓剑`.
- Phase 2: shield removed, original two-handed sword stance, exposed curse fissures and torn mantle; the same sword gains restrained cold-blue blade fissures.
- Sword construction: sharp two-stage taper, pale edge, old-silver plane, dark shadow plane, central ridge, gate-arch/raven-wing guard, wrapped long grip and faceted oath-seal pommel. No runtime frame may replace it with a line or the superseded short blade.
- Dedicated turn, block, break, phase transition, charge, combo, overhead, jump-smash, shockwave, hurt and death art preserves all existing Boss contracts.
- See `chapter_01_boss_art_bible.md` for phase and silhouette rules.
