# Chapter I Environment Storytelling Specification

Implementation: `res://chapters/chapter_01_ravenmourn_outskirts/scripts/level/chapter_one_storytelling_art.gd`

Visual landmarks communicate the failed Veiled Order expedition without interaction prompts or exposition pickups. All marks are native 2D draw calls and have no collision, process loop, gameplay signal, or state authority.

| Approx. x | Landmark | Intended reading |
|---:|---|---|
| 740 | Fallen cloak and paired daggers | A warden died on the route. |
| 1480 | Broken warning post | The road was deliberately sealed or marked. |
| 2650 | Empty cursed armor | The curse persists without a body. |
| 3520 | Spearman remains | The castle's defenders fell or transformed. |
| 4260 | Broken camp and cold fire | A later expedition failed to hold ground. |
| 4920 | Pale Soul Mark fragments | The protagonist's condition and castle are linked. |
| route-wide | Crows | Ravenmourn watches and pulls the player east. |

The layer complements, rather than replaces, the existing forest, frontier, approach, castle, bridge, moat, and gate art.
