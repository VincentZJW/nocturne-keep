# The Night Warden — Protagonist Narrative Specification

The Night Warden (夜巡守卫) is a young Veilbound / 夜誓者 of the Veiled Order / 暮帷会, trained for fast infiltration and close dual-dagger combat. Seven years ago they joined the Order's final entry into Ravenmourn Castle. Nobody returned. The protagonist now rises from death with fractured memories and no revealed true name.

## Readable identity

- Black hood and concealed face: the self is present but inaccessible.
- Dual daggers: professional Order training, not improvised heroism.
- Soul Mark / 魂契印: a broken silver seal, the source of the ghost released on death and the force pulling it back at a checkpoint.
- Controlled movement: calm competence contrasts with missing memory.

## Chapter I arc

The protagonist begins by following a summons they cannot fully explain. Environmental remains imply they knew the fallen wardens. The Gate Knight's final line confirms that the castle—or the bell within it—recognizes them. Chapter I ends before that recognition is explained.

No dialogue choice, morality system, inventory revelation, or new ability unlock is introduced in this milestone.
