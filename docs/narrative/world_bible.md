# Veil of Ravenmourn / 《鸦泣之帷》 World Bible

## Core premise

Valendor / 瓦伦多尔王国 is being consumed by a curse originating beneath Ravenmourn Castle / 鸦泣城堡. Seven years before Chapter I, **The Hollow Bell / 无声之钟** rang thirteen times during **The Night of Hollow Bells / 空钟之夜**. Ordinary people cannot hear it; only the dying and the dead can. Castle guards became undead, the forest rotted, the moat turned cold blue, armies vanished, and the curse began spreading into the kingdom.

The Veiled Order / 暮帷会 sent its last team of **Veilbound / 夜誓者** into the castle. None returned. The protagonist was among them. Chapter I begins when that young dual-dagger warden rises from death with most memories gone and a broken silver **Soul Mark / 魂契印** still binding spirit to body.

The protagonist's body was deliberately preserved for seven years upon **The Severed Altar / 断魂祭坛** inside **Veilbound Catacomb / 暮帷墓窟**, an Order burial chamber beneath the Dark Forest. A surviving custodian known only as **The Candle Warden / 守烛人** tends its blue soul flames and knows that the Soul Mark did not restore life; it dragged the spirit back into its former body. The Candle Warden sends the protagonist toward Ravenmourn but conceals who preserved the corpse, why the bell remembers the protagonist, and what truly returned from the castle seven years ago.

## Factions and terms

- **Valendor / 瓦伦多尔王国**: the realm threatened by the spreading curse.
- **Ravenmourn Castle / 鸦泣古堡**: fortress, reliquary, and source of the current curse.
- **The Hollow Bell / 无声之钟**: the inaudible ancient bell sealed beneath the castle.
- **The Veiled Order / 暮帷会**: the secret order that sent the final expedition.
- **Veilbound / 夜誓者**: members of the Veiled Order, including the protagonist.
- **Soul Mark / 魂契印**: a broken silver seal that prevents the protagonist's soul from leaving the world.
- **Veilbound Catacomb / 暮帷墓窟**: the Veiled Order's damaged funerary chamber and Chapter I revival threshold.
- **The Severed Altar / 断魂祭坛**: the soul-contract altar on which the protagonist's preserved body is restored.
- **The Candle Warden / 守烛人**: an unnamed keeper of the dead, blue lantern, and catacomb gate; allegiance and nature remain ambiguous.

## Narrative rules

1. The protagonist's identity is a mystery, not a blank personality.
2. The Black Bell is an active intelligence or conduit; its exact nature remains unrevealed.
3. Chapter I communicates mainly through silhouettes, remains, architecture, and short subtitles.
4. Enemies do not drop exposition. Their equipment and placement show what happened.
5. Each resurrection is not a blessing: the Soul Mark pulls the spirit back and may draw it closer to something inside the castle.
6. The first gate is a threshold, not a victory screen. The chapter ends with entry, not celebration.

## Chapter I player knowledge

The surviving memory fragments are Ravenmourn Castle, a faceless woman, an opened underground stone door, the black ancient bell, bloodied hands, and the accusation “You opened that door.” By the gate, the player should wonder whether the protagonist returned to stop the curse or caused it. The player should not yet know who the woman is or whether the Soul Mark is protection or ownership.
