## Appearance and presentation

The Candle Warden is tall and narrow beneath a patched charcoal robe, hood and dull metal face covering. A small catacomb key hangs at the belt. The only bright feature is an old cage lantern containing restrained blue soul fire. There is no visible combat weapon. Story presentation states are `SEATED`, `RISING`, `IDLE`, `WALK`, `RAISE_LANTERN`, `TALK`, and `TURN_AWAY`; the NPC owns no Health, Hitbox, Hurtbox, AI chase or forest-follow behavior.

## Personality and function

Speech is economical, funerary and deliberately incomplete. The Warden acts as keeper rather than rescuer: they confirm the seven-year interval, distinguish soul return from life, identify the Hollow Bell objective, and open the physical threshold only after the protagonist takes the blades.

## Knowledge boundaries

Known and stated: the Soul Mark pulled the spirit back; the bell remembers the protagonist; the Fallen Gate Knight guards the bridge; the protagonist is the only expedition member who returned.

Known or suspected but withheld: who ordered the corpse preserved, why the Mark refuses release, what the protagonist did inside Ravenmourn, whether the Warden is living, and why the fourteenth toll matters. The first chapter displays only the title “守烛人 / Candle Warden”; no true name is exposed.
