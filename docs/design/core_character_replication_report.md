# Core Character Replication Report

## Scope

This pass formalizes the two shared actors that appear across the Prologue and Chapters I–III:

- The Night Warden player in Veilbound, Ravenfang and Crimson Masque presentations.
- The Candle Warden narrative NPC.

It preserves approved concepts, animation timing, collision, gameplay anchors and Main routing. Only formal runtime presentation assets and their deterministic exporters were changed.

## Night Warden

- Formal runtime canvas: 96×96 transparent PNG, centered from the established 64×64 action drawings.
- Shared foot and body origin are unchanged; extra canvas is symmetric transparent padding.
- The pointed hood seam, paired eye glints, oath clasp and bracer separation are reinforced on every living action pose.
- Veilbound keeps moonlit slate and muted amber; Ravenfang uses an oxidized crimson clasp; Crimson Masque uses a subdued masque-red clasp.
- All 30 animation families and all three equipment presentations are regenerated by the same exporter.

## Candle Warden

- Formal runtime canvas: 128×128 transparent PNG, centered from the established 80×80 narrative drawings.
- The segmented funerary mask, asymmetrical soul-lit eye slit, oath-key chain, wax seal and reinforced robe hem are persistent.
- All 15 dialogue, locomotion and cinematic animation families are regenerated.
- Existing lantern light, soul flame, interaction sequencing and story logic are unchanged.
