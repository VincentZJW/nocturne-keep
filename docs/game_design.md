# Game Design Baseline

Version: 0.1.0 (M0)
Status: scope baseline; no gameplay implemented

## Design pillars

1. **Readable commitment** — attacks and evasions have clear timing, anticipation, and recovery.
2. **Compact discovery** — three rooms teach, test, unlock, and recontextualize one route within 5–10 minutes.
3. **Earned mobility** — defeating the Iron Warden unlocks double jump and visibly opens a previously unreachable path.
4. **Learnable danger** — enemies and the Bell Keeper use patterns the player can observe and counter.

## Core loop

Explore → fight → reach checkpoint → defeat elite → unlock double jump → backtrack → enter boss arena → win.

## Player experience targets

- The opening corridor gives a safe first success with movement and jumping.
- The guard hall introduces combat and establishes the unreachable elevated route.
- The bell-tower shaft tests vertical control before the elite encounter.
- Returning with double jump should feel like mastery rather than a key check hidden in UI.
- Boss phase two raises tempo while preserving readable and avoidable attack windows.

## Scope guardrails

No equipment, skill tree, inventory, shop, multiplayer, procedural layout, extra boss, or fourth main room. M0 contains no mechanics to specify or balance. Numerical gameplay values remain the Master Prompt's hypotheses until their implementation milestone and playtest.
